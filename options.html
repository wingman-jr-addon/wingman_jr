Which zone should be the default?

This addon uses the concept of "zones" to indicate how strictly it filters images.

Show an on/off switch on the main menu?

Add a switch to turn filtering on and off quickly while browsing. This also controls the Reveal Blocked Image menu visibility. Note: already-blocked images will only reappear when the cache is cleared - hit shift+refresh on the page to reload it fully.

Video blocking?

Video blocking requires quite a bit of processing, and the accuracy is currently lower due to technical limitations. Additionally, not all video sites are supported because video data is delivered to the browser in many ways.

Whitelist and blacklist image/video URLs

Whitelist matches are allowed without scanning. Blacklist matches are always blocked. If both lists match, the blacklist wins. These rules apply to the requested image or video URL, which may use a different domain than the page. Tip: right-click an image to add its host or a broader parent domain.

One per line. Domains include their subdomains. Lines beginning with # are notes.
