Settings protection

Use one password to guard filtering controls, site exceptions, URL lists, and the rest of Wingman Jr.'s settings.

Settings are currently open and can be changed without a password.

Protect these settings?

After protection is enabled, the settings page stays hidden until the password is entered. It locks again when you leave or reload this page.

  • Filtering on/off and Smart Pause
  • Site zones and URL whitelist or blacklist rules
  • Audit, filtering model, and other add-on settings

Keep the password somewhere safe. A forgotten-password reset removes protection, turns audit off, and erases saved audit history.

Create the settings password

Enter the same password twice. It must be at least eight characters.

Settings are locked.

Remove password protection?

This makes all settings immediately editable without a password. It does not turn audit off, erase audit history, or change your current filtering choices.

Reset a forgotten password?

This removes password protection, turns audit off, and permanently erases saved audit history and blocked-image previews. Your current filtering settings remain in place.

Filtering

Default site zone

Sites without an override use this zone. Adaptive automatically changes zones based on how many unsafe images have been detected recently.

Video blocking?

Video blocking requires quite a bit of processing, and the accuracy is currently lower due to technical limitations. Additionally, not all video sites are supported because video data is delivered to the browser in many ways.

All video scans combine up to four frames into each low-resolution ML scan. Quick Scan performs one combined scan and then lets the rest of the video flow through. It is the default mode.

Site filtering overrides

These zones apply to top-level page hostnames, not the domains serving individual images or videos. Adaptive is an explicit site override; Use default follows the Default site zone selection above.

    No sites have custom filtering settings.

    Whitelist and blacklist image/video URLs

    When filtering is enabled for a site, whitelist matches are allowed without scanning and blacklist matches are blocked. If both lists match, the blacklist wins. These rules apply to the requested image or video URL, which may use a different domain than the page. Tip: right-click an image to add its host or a broader parent domain.

    Show on/off controls on the main menu?

    Show controls for pausing filtering everywhere, including Smart Pause timers, or persistently turning it off for the current site. The Off site card is hidden with these controls; the other zone cards remain available. This also controls the Reveal Blocked Image menu visibility.

    Audit history

    Optionally keep a local, reduced history of filtering scores, important changes, private browsing coverage, and tiny thumbnails of blocked images.

    Privacy: Audit history intentionally stores page-site domains and browsing-derived information on this device, including activity from private windows when Firefox allows Wingman Jr. to run there.

    Turning audit off permanently erases the saved audit history and blocked-image previews.
    Private browsing is not included. Someone can open a Private Window to browse without that activity appearing in Audit. To close this gap, open Wingman Jr. in Firefox Add-ons and set Run in Private Windows to Allow.
